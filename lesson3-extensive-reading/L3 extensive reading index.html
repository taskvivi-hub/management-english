From Launch to Investment

You have a great idea for a new product.

你有一個很棒的新產品構想。

You already know some important steps for launching it.

你已經知道推出新產品的一些重要步驟。

You need to design attractive packaging, decide on your target audience, do market research, and study your competition.

你需要設計吸引人的包裝、決定目標受眾、進行市場研究，並研究競爭情況。

However, there is one big problem.

然而，有一個很大的問題。

Launching a new product costs money.

推出新產品需要花錢。

A company may need money for materials, production, websites, and advertising.

公司可能需要資金來支付材料、生產、網站和廣告等費用。

So where can a young company get the money it needs?

那麼，一家年輕的公司可以從哪裡取得所需的資金呢？

For many new companies, one possible answer is venture capital.

對許多新公司來說，其中一個可能的答案就是創業投資。

What Is Venture Capital?

A startup is a new company that wants to grow quickly.

新創公司是希望快速成長的新公司。

Venture capital (VC) is money that investors give to startups that they believe may become successful.

創業投資是投資人提供給他們認為可能成功的新創公司的資金。

These investors are called venture capitalists, or VCs.

這些投資人稱為創業投資人，也可簡稱為 VCs。

Getting money from a VC is different from borrowing money from a bank.

從創投取得資金與向銀行借款不同。

A bank usually asks a company to pay the money back.

銀行通常要求公司償還借款。

A VC usually receives equity instead.

創投通常會改為取得公司的股權。

Equity means that the investor owns part of the company.

股權代表投資人擁有公司的一部分。

If the company becomes successful, the value of the investor's part may increase.

如果公司成功，投資人所持有部分的價值可能會提高。

The investor may then earn a good return on investment (ROI).

投資人之後可能獲得良好的投資報酬。

ROI shows how much money an investor earns from an investment.

投資報酬顯示投資人從一項投資中賺到多少錢。

Silicon Valley in California is famous for technology startups and venture capital.

美國加州的矽谷以科技新創公司和創業投資聞名。

One well-known VC company is Sequoia Capital.

Sequoia Capital 是一家知名的創投公司。

In 1999, when Google was still a small company, Sequoia Capital and another VC company invested 25 million US dollars in it.

1999 年，當 Google 還是一家小公司時，Sequoia Capital 和另一家創投公司共同投資了 2,500 萬美元。

Google later became one of the world's most valuable companies, so this early investment became very successful.

Google 後來成為全球最有價值的公司之一，因此這筆早期投資也非常成功。

1. A Strong Management Team

VCs receive many business ideas, but they invest in only a small number of companies.

創投會收到許多商業構想，但只會投資其中少數公司。

Investors do not only look at a business idea.

投資人不只會看商業構想。

They also look carefully at the people behind it.

他們也會仔細觀察背後的團隊成員。

A startup needs a team with useful skills and expertise.

新創公司需要一個具備實用技能與專業能力的團隊。

Expertise means special knowledge or skill in an area.

Expertise 指的是某個領域中的專業知識或技能。

For example, a technology startup needs people who understand the technology.

例如，科技新創公司需要真正了解技術的人才。

A good team should also be able to work together, share ideas, and solve problems.

好的團隊也應該能夠合作、分享想法並解決問題。

Different people may have different opinions, but they should be able to discuss them and make good decisions together.

不同的人可能有不同的意見，但他們應該能夠一起討論並做出好的決定。

2. A Unique Product

A product should have a clear unique selling point (USP).

產品應該有清楚的獨特賣點。

This is something special that makes the product different from its competitors.

這是讓產品與競爭產品不同的特殊之處。

This is why studying the competition is important.

這就是為什麼研究競爭情況很重要。

If another company can easily copy the product, the startup may lose its advantage.

如果其他公司很容易模仿這項產品，新創公司可能會失去優勢。

A company should also think carefully about its product features.

公司也應該仔細思考產品特色。

More features are not always better because every new feature can increase the cost.

更多功能不一定更好，因為每增加一項功能都可能提高成本。

A smart company focuses on the features that customers really need.

聰明的公司會專注在顧客真正需要的功能上。

3. A Growing Market

Investors also want to know who will buy the product.

投資人也想知道誰會購買這項產品。

They want to see a clear target audience and a market that can grow.

他們希望看到清楚的目標受眾，以及具有成長可能的市場。

Many startups first sell to a small group of customers.

許多新創公司一開始會先銷售給一小群顧客。

Later, they may expand to other groups with similar needs.

之後，他們可能擴展到具有類似需求的其他客群。

As a company sells more products, it may increase its market share.

當公司銷售更多產品時，它可能提高自己的市場占有率。

Market share means the part of total sales in a market that belongs to one company.

市場占有率是指某家公司在整個市場總銷售中所占的比例。

Market research can help a startup understand what consumers want.

市場研究可以幫助新創公司了解消費者想要什麼。

Focus groups, surveys, and other research can show whether customers care most about price, features, design, or ease of use.

焦點團體、問卷和其他研究可以顯示顧客最在意價格、功能、設計，還是使用便利性。

4. A Great Business Plan

Before investing, VCs often want to see a business plan.

在投資之前，創投通常會想先看公司的商業計畫。

A business plan explains the product, the target customers, the costs, and the company's goals.

商業計畫會說明產品、目標顧客、成本，以及公司的目標。

It may also include sales forecasts, which are predictions about how much the company expects to sell.

它也可能包含銷售預測，也就是公司預期能賣出多少產品的預測。

Investors study these numbers carefully.

投資人會仔細研究這些數字。

If a company gives a forecast that is too high or unrealistic, investors may lose trust.

如果公司提出過高或不實際的預測，投資人可能會失去信任。

Realistic numbers are usually more convincing.

實際合理的數字通常更有說服力。

A company should think about its budget, its production ability, and the real size of the market.

公司應該考量預算、生產能力，以及市場的實際規模。

5. Coachable Founders

Investors also look for founders who are coachable.

投資人也會尋找願意接受指導的創辦人。

This means they are willing to listen to advice and learn from other people.

這表示他們願意聽取建議並向其他人學習。

Experienced investors may give useful suggestions.

有經驗的投資人可能會提供有用的建議。

For example, they may suggest a Beta test before a full product launch.

例如，他們可能建議在正式全面上市前先進行 Beta 測試。

During a Beta test, a company sells or tests the product in a smaller market first.

在 Beta 測試期間，公司會先在較小的市場銷售或測試產品。

This can help the company find problems and collect feedback.

這可以幫助公司發現問題並收集回饋。

Founders who listen to useful advice and improve their product can show investors that they are ready to learn and grow.

願意聽取實用建議並改善產品的創辦人，可以向投資人展現自己已準備好學習與成長。

Money, Equity, and a Seat at the Table

VCs often give startups more than money.

創投提供給新創公司的往往不只是資金。

They may also provide business connections and managerial expertise.

他們也可能提供商業人脈和管理方面的專業知識。

Managerial expertise means knowledge and skill in running a business.

管理專業指的是經營企業所需的知識與能力。

In return, the investor may ask for equity.

作為回報，投資人可能會要求取得股權。

Sometimes the investor may also ask for a board seat.

有時投資人也可能要求一個董事席位。

A board seat gives the investor a voice in important company decisions.

董事席位讓投資人能對公司的重要決策表達意見。

Before accepting an investment, founders should understand the agreement carefully.

在接受投資之前，創辦人應該仔細了解投資協議。

They should know how much equity the investor will receive and what the board seat allows the investor to do.

他們應該知道投資人會取得多少股權，以及董事席位讓投資人擁有哪些權利。

Investors also think about an exit strategy.

投資人也會思考退出策略。

This is a plan for how they will finally earn money from their investment.

這是他們最終如何從投資中獲利的計畫。

For example, the company may be sold to a larger company.

例如，公司可能被出售給一家更大的公司。

Another possibility is that shares of the company may later be sold to the public.

另一種可能是公司的股份之後公開出售給大眾。

Putting It All Together

Launching a product and finding an investor are closely connected.

推出產品和尋找投資人其實密切相關。

The same things that can make a product launch successful are also important to investors.

能讓產品成功上市的條件，對投資人來說也同樣重要。

A startup needs to understand its customers, study its competition, test its product, and make a realistic plan.

新創公司需要了解顧客、研究競爭情況、測試產品，並制定實際可行的計畫。

Investors also want to see a strong team, a unique product, a growing market, and founders who are willing to learn.

投資人也希望看到強大的團隊、獨特的產品、正在成長的市場，以及願意學習的創辦人。

If a startup can show these strengths, it may have a better chance of receiving the money it needs to bring its idea to life.

如果新創公司能展現這些優勢，就更有機會獲得所需資金，把構想真正實現。
